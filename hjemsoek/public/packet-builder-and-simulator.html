Model notes

What this prototype counts.

Every enabled packet is emitted at its configured packet rate. “Per client” multiplies that stream by the connected-client count. A lost packet still consumes outbound bandwidth; delivered bandwidth is reported separately in the results.

Delay, loss, and jitter are sampled independently. Congestion, queue coupling, ordered delivery, retransmission, acknowledgements, packet coalescing, and bandwidth caps are not simulated.

Netcode guide

A plain-language map of packets, bytes, rates, routes, and simulation results.

Start here

A packet is a small, ordered message sent across the network.

Packet tabs define exactly which values become bytes and in which order. Simulation tabs then repeat those packets for one or more clients and apply illustrative latency, jitter, extra delay, and loss.

Throughout the application, hover, focus, or click a ? for the explanation that applies to that control.
